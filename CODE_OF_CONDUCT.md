# Code of Conduct

This repository adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, by reference. The full text is at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html and applies here.

## Our pledge

We want everyone who takes part to be able to do so free of harassment, whatever their background or experience.

## Our standards

Expected: respectful and patient discussion, criticism aimed at the work and not the person, and evidence for claims about the study.

Not accepted: insults, personal attacks, harassment, publishing someone's private information, and anything else that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these standards, and may block the people who write them.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile, https://github.com/gsalucci. Reports are treated as private.
